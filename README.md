# SFLPRS Records Archive

Photographed minute books and records of the **San Francisco Ladies’ Protection and Relief Society** (Heritage on the Marina), 1906–2020. The pages were photographed on a Czur scanner in eight sessions, March–September 2025, and organized into this archive in October 2026.

The Society was founded in San Francisco in 1853 and today runs Heritage on the Marina. More: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Ladies_Protection_and_Relief_Society) · [heritageonthemarina.org](https://heritageonthemarina.org/)

**Browse and search:** https://sflprs.github.io

## How to use it

- **Online:** open https://sflprs.github.io. Pick a volume on the left, or type in the search box to find words in the typed text of every page. Click a page to see it full size; use ◀ ▶ or the arrow keys to turn pages, 📝 to show the machine-read text, and ↻ to rotate pages that were photographed sideways.
- **From a downloaded copy:** download or clone each repository, keeping its name, and put the folders side by side in one folder and open `sflprs.github.io/index.html` in a browser. No internet connection or installation is needed.
- **Find a file:** `manifest.csv` here lists every page in every repository, with its original scan file name, checksum and the date found on the page.

## Volumes

3,468 page images in 25 volumes. Volume titles follow the spine or cover labels in the photos (shortened to a common pattern); where no label was photographed, the dates come from the minutes. “Dates found in text” is the range of meeting dates the OCR picked up, so it can be narrower than the book’s real span, especially for handwritten volumes. “Scanned from” gives the original scan folder names as they were, including the typo “Dau 4”.

## How the files were handled

- Every image is an exact copy of an original photo (same bytes; MD5 checksums in `manifest.csv` and `checksums-md5.txt`). Only the file names and folders are new.
- **3,468 unique photos** were kept. **631 exact duplicates were left out** and are listed in `duplicates-not-copied.csv`: the 626 loose files in `Heritage Minutes Dau 4` (identical to its `2025_03_17_12_25_34` subfolder) and 5 top-level files identical to pages in `Heritage Minutes Day 3`.
- Not included: two 2020 software installers found in the `Czur` scan folder, and zip backups of the scan folders (checked: they contain no photos beyond these).
- **First-day file names:** the top-level scan files `image00001–00004.jpg` and `image00272.jpg` from 2025-03-03 hold Day 3 pictures, so the first-day photos with those names were probably overwritten. `Day 1 Main Ledger.jpg` and `image00272 Day 1 Ledger 3.jpg` look like two of them, renamed. The ledger’s numbered first pages are all present.

## Things to know

- **Text is machine-read (OCR) and contains errors.** Typed pages read reasonably well. **Handwritten pages (most of T1, C1, A1, A2 and the 1953 guest book) are not searchable.** 378 pages photographed sideways were read again after turning them.
- **Dates** are the first date found near the top of each page — a guide, not a catalog. A blank means no date was found.
- **Possible re-shots:** 139 pages closely match another page’s text (`possible_reshot_of` in `manifest.csv`). Most are the same page photographed twice; all were kept. Part B of C2 is an earlier, partial pass of the same book.
- **Board of Managers vs. Board of Directors:** M4 (1977–80) is labelled “Board of Directors” on its cover, but it is the women’s board, which briefly used that name. The modern Board of Directors volumes (2000–2020) are a separate series.
- **Gaps:** no Trustees volumes were photographed for 1956–68; no Board of Managers meeting minutes before 1954 (only the 1941–55 annual reports) or for 1969–71, 1985–88 or 1992–95; and no Board of Directors volumes after 2020.
